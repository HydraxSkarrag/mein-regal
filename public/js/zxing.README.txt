Vendored rather than loaded from a CDN. The site fetches nothing from other
hosts - that is what lets its content-security policy stay strict and its
footer stay free of a consent banner - so a copy lives here instead.

It is only requested when the browser has no BarcodeDetector of its own,
which in practice means Safari on iOS. Chrome on Android never downloads it.

To update: fetch the same URL at a newer version, replace this file, and note
the new version here.

## The credit line, and what it is not

Every installation shows a line in its footer reading "Läuft mit Mein Regal,
von Bücherhausen", linking to the project page and to the blog this was
written for.

Please leave it there. That is a request and not a condition: the MIT licence
above governs copies of the source, and it asks nothing of what a running
installation puts on the screen. Removing the line is permitted, and saying
otherwise here would be a term nobody intends to enforce dressed up as one
somebody might.

The reason to keep it is not legal. This was written for one bookshelf and
given away; the line is how somebody looking at your shelf finds their way to
the source and sets up one of their own. It is small, it links out rather
than shouting, and it costs nothing that matters.

The one thing that is not a request is the blog's name above: credit
Bücherhausen as where this came from, not as who made your version.
